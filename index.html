About the Centre

Redgum Tutoring has been operating from 2/112 Limestone Street, Ipswich since 2017. The centre was founded by Helen Vasquez, a former secondary mathematics teacher with fourteen years of classroom experience.

We currently have around 140 active students and eleven tutors. Sessions are one-on-one and run for either sixty or ninety minutes.
